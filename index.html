What We Do in EHRP

About EHRP

Emergency Hamburg Roleplay (EHRP) is a realistic Roblox roleplay community based on Emergency Hamburg. Players can patrol as Law Enforcement, respond to emergencies as Fire & Rescue, service vehicles, drive buses/trucks, or act as civilians and criminals.

Server Startup (SSU) Guide

What is an SSU? Server Startup (SSU) is when our staff opens up private servers for scheduled, high-quality roleplay sessions.

When is SSU? SSU announcements, start times, and server codes are posted live in our Discord server under the #ssu-announcements channel.
